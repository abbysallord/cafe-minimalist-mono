Our Ethos

Crafted with intention, served with grace.

01

Direct Trade Sourcing

We partner directly with family-owned micro-lots across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra, ensuring absolute traceability and ethical compensation.

02

Precision Roasting

Each bean is roasted in small batches on our vintage cast-iron roaster, highlighting unique origin notes, acidity, and delicate floral finishes.

03

Mindful Ritual

Our space is crafted as a sanctuary from the rush. Every pour-over and ceramic vessel is designed to invite stillness and contemplation.
